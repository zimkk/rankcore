# Research Workflow

Use for `/rank research`.

## Product first

Infer:
- category;
- audience;
- buyer/user;
- capabilities;
- geography/language;
- business model;
- differentiation evidence.

Load `intent-content.md`, `search-foundations.md`, `authority-citations.md`, and `guardrails.md`.

## Current research when browsing exists

Research:
- how the category is described by users and competitors;
- representative SERPs/search-result patterns;
- intent clusters;
- alternatives/comparisons;
- use cases;
- integrations;
- support/troubleshooting demand;
- informational questions;
- relevant communities and citation opportunities;
- current search/AI-search platform guidance when necessary.

## Evidence discipline

Separate:
- observed evidence;
- inferred opportunity;
- unverified hypothesis.

Never fabricate:
- search volume;
- keyword difficulty;
- traffic;
- backlink counts;
- rank positions;
- market share.

Provider metrics are allowed only when the user actually supplies/connects a provider and the provider/date are named.

Persist durable conclusions to `.rankcore/research.md`.
