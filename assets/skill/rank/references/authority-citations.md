# Authority, Links, Citations, and Promotion

Verified: 2026-09-21  
Review-after: 2027-03-21  
Authority: official-docs + internal synthesis

## Principle

Authority is not a link-count contest. Build independent reasons for the product/site to be referenced by people, publications, communities, directories, partners, customers, and search/answer systems.

## Sustainable link earning

Useful assets include:
- original research/data;
- open-source tools;
- calculators;
- templates;
- technical documentation;
- benchmark studies;
- public datasets;
- expert commentary;
- genuinely useful free utilities;
- integration resources;
- high-quality guides with original evidence.

Promotion is legitimate. Manipulative link creation is not.

## Digital PR

Good digital PR:
- identifies a real newsworthy/data angle;
- targets relevant journalists/creators;
- provides verifiable facts and methodology;
- gives clean source material;
- accepts that coverage is editorially independent.

Do not recommend mass AI press-release spam for backlinks.

## Citations and mentions

Track multiple forms of independent visibility:
- editorial links;
- unlinked brand mentions;
- review/profile listings;
- industry directories with real users;
- conference/association references;
- community references;
- product ecosystems;
- academic/technical references where relevant.

A citation can be valuable even when it does not pass ranking credit.

## Communities

Reddit, forums, GitHub, Stack Overflow, niche communities, Slack/Discord groups, and social platforms can reveal vocabulary and real problems.

Participate transparently. Do not automate fake grassroots recommendations or spam links.

## Partnerships

Potential legitimate sources:
- integration partners;
- technology ecosystems;
- customers/case studies with permission;
- associations;
- marketplaces;
- vendor directories;
- speakers/events;
- open-source dependencies/contributions.

Never require unqualified ranking links as a contractual condition.

## Link policy guardrails

Avoid:
- buying/selling links for ranking credit;
- automated link networks;
- excessive reciprocal schemes;
- low-quality directory blasts;
- forum/comment signature spam;
- paid guest-post networks;
- keyword-rich distributed widget/footer links;
- expired-domain acquisition purely for inherited ranking signals.

Paid/sponsored links should use appropriate qualification where applicable.

## Measuring authority work

Do not use DA/DR as if they were Google metrics.

If using commercial backlink datasets:
- name provider;
- date the snapshot;
- distinguish live/estimated data;
- avoid cross-provider comparisons as if metrics are identical.

Track outcomes such as:
- quality referring sources;
- relevant mentions;
- referral traffic;
- branded demand;
- qualified leads;
- actual cited resources.

## AI citations

There is no guaranteed recipe for answer-engine citation.

Improve citation readiness through:
- crawlable public pages;
- precise claims;
- source transparency;
- clear entity identity;
- original evidence;
- concise explanations;
- stable URLs;
- strong reputation across the web.

Do not buy “AI mentions” or fabricate third-party references.
